🗒️Summary Findings 

1. Product Category Top Performers Findings:

Roasted coffee and tea are the strongest revenue drivers, with bakery contributing steadily as a supporting category.

•Coffee☕
•Tea🫖
•Bakery 🍰


2. Lowest Performing Product Category – Packaged Chocolate is the weakest product category overall

3. Total Revenue per Store Location:
Findings: 🔥Hell’s Kitchen generates the highest revenue, driven by strong coffee, tea, and bakery sales.

4. Top Performing Product Types Across Store Locations:
Findings: Barista Espresso, Gourmet Boxed Coffee, and Hot Chocolate are consistent revenue drivers across all three branches.

5. Time Bucket Overview per Store Location:
Findings: Morning consistently generates the highest revenue across all branches. Afternoon and evening are moderate, while night is weakest.

6. Hell’s Kitchen Sales Time Interval & Astoria Weekly Sales
Hell’s Kitchen: Morning is the most profitable time, night is the weakest.
Astoria: Wednesday is the peak sales day, outperforming other weekdays.

7. Total Revenue per Month
Findings: Revenue rises steadily from January to June, peaking in June (R166,465). The sharp increase from March to April shows strong seasonal demand.
